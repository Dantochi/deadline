Delete your account

Deadline (Android and iPhone)

An account only exists if you set up the accountability partner. Deleting it removes your sign-in, username, display name, partnership and weekly summary from our servers. Tasks and notes are kept only on your phone and are not affected unless you erase them there.

In the app (instant)

  1. Open Deadline and tap the settings icon on the Life screen.
  2. Open Accountability partner.
  3. Tap Delete account, enter your password and tap Delete for good.

Without the app

Email REPLACE_WITH_SUPPORT_EMAIL from the address you signed up with and ask us to delete your account. We delete the account and all data listed above within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your Firebase sign-in (email and password).
  • Your username and display name.
  • Your partnership and any pending requests.
  • Your weekly summary.

Nothing is kept after deletion, apart from records we are legally required to keep (none at present).

Data on your phone

To remove your tasks, notes and settings, use Settings → Erase everything, or uninstall the app.
