Privacy policy

Last updated 29 October 2026

Deadline is built so that almost everything stays on your phone. This page explains what the app stores, what leaves your phone, and the choices you have.

Stored only on your phone

  • Your date of birth and reference age, used to draw your life grid.
  • Your tasks, deadlines, reserved times, notes, backup plans, cues and check-in answers.
  • Your settings, saved quotes and reminder times.

This information is not sent to us. Uninstalling the app, or using Settings → Erase everything, deletes it.

Only if you use the accountability partner

The partner feature is optional and needs an account. If you create one, these are stored with Google Firebase: sign-in by Firebase Authentication, and the rest in Cloud Firestore, hosted in London (UK):

  • Your email address and password, used only to sign in. Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication; we never see them. Your email is never shown to other people.
  • Your username and display name, so your partner can find and recognise you.
  • Your partnership: who you asked or accepted.
  • A weekly summary that only your active partner can read: sessions kept and missed, tasks finished, how many tasks are on track or need attention, and, if you allow it, task titles. Notes are never shared.

Permissions

  • Notifications: reminders are scheduled on your phone. No reminder content is sent to a server.
  • Photos (save only): used when you save a wallpaper image of your life grid. The app does not read your photo library.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, no ad tracking and no selling or sharing of personal data.
  • No analytics or crash reporting tools are included in the current version.

Your choices

  • Use the app without an account; everything except the partner feature works offline.
  • Turn off task titles in Settings → Accountability partner to share counts only.
  • Delete your account and all shared data in the app (Settings → Accountability partner → Delete account), or see Delete your account.

Children

Deadline is not directed at children under 13.
