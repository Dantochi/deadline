Privacy policy

Last updated 17 November 2026

Deadline is built so that almost everything stays on your phone. This page explains what the app stores, what leaves your phone, and the choices you have.

Stored only on your phone

  • Your date of birth and reference age, used to draw your life grid.
  • Your tasks, deadlines, reserved times, notes, backup plans, cues and check-in answers.
  • Your settings, saved quotes and reminder times.

This information is not sent to us. If you turn on backup & sync (below), an end-to-end encrypted copy is kept in your account, which we cannot read. Uninstalling the app, or using Settings → Erase everything, deletes what is on the phone.

Only if you use the accountability partner

The partner feature is optional and needs an account. If you create one, these are stored with Google Firebase: sign-in by Firebase Authentication, and the rest in Cloud Firestore, hosted in London (UK):

  • Your email address and password, used only to sign in. Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication; we never see them. Your email is never shown to other people.
  • Your username and display name, so your partner can find and recognise you.
  • Your partnership: who you asked or accepted.
  • A weekly summary that only your active partner can read: sessions kept and missed, tasks finished, how many tasks are on track or need attention, and, if you allow it, task titles. Notes are never shared.

Only if you turn on backup & sync

Backup & sync is off unless you turn it on (Settings → Backup & sync). It uses the same account as the partner feature. When it is on, your tasks, notes, important dates and settings are copied to Cloud Firestore so you can restore them on a new phone and keep several phones in step.

  • End-to-end encrypted. Everything is encrypted on your phone before it is sent, with a key that never leaves your devices. We, Google and anyone else with access to the servers see only scrambled data, plus when each item last changed and how many there are.
  • Your recovery phrase (24 words) is shown to you once and is not stored anywhere readable. With it, or with approval from another of your phones, you can unlock your backup on a new phone. If you lose all your phones and the phrase, nobody can recover the backup, including us.
  • Turning it off on a phone stops syncing there. Deleting your backup, or your account, removes the encrypted copy.

Only if you use dictation in notes

When you hold or tap the microphone button in a note, the recording is sent to our server and on to AssemblyAI, which turns it into text and tidies it (fillers, punctuation, corrections). The text comes back to your phone. Recordings are kept in memory only for the request and are not stored by our server. AssemblyAI's own handling is described in its privacy policy.

Only if you turn on "Understand with Gemini"

This setting is off unless you turn it on (Settings → Voice). When it is on, the sentence you say or type to create a task, together with today's date, is sent to Google's Gemini model through Firebase AI Logic so it can pick out the deadline, days, times, length and subtasks. Nothing else is sent: not your other tasks, notes, birth date or account. Deadline does not store these sentences on a server. Google processes them under the Gemini API terms; while Deadline uses Google's free tier, those terms let Google use what is sent to improve its products, so avoid saying anything private in a task sentence. With the setting off, sentences are read only on your phone.

Only if you set up "Wisdom for your work"

This is optional (during setup or in Settings → Wisdom). When you use it, the career you type (or the one you are studying for) and the names of creators you pick are sent to Google's Gemini model through Firebase AI Logic, to check the career and suggest well-known people in that field. Nothing else is sent. Careers that harm others are refused on your phone before anything is sent. Links on the Wisdom tab open ordinary YouTube or Google searches in your browser or the YouTube app.

Permissions

  • Notifications: reminders are scheduled on your phone. No reminder content is sent to a server.
  • Photos (save only): used when you save a wallpaper image of your life grid. The app does not read your photo library.
  • Microphone (only while the mic is on): when you say a task, your phone's own speech service turns it into text. On many phones that service is run by Google or Apple and may process the audio on their servers. Deadline receives only the text, reads the dates and times from it on your phone (or with Gemini, if you turned that on), and never stores or sends audio.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, no ad tracking and no selling or sharing of personal data.
  • No analytics or crash reporting tools are included in the current version.

Your choices

  • Use the app without an account; everything except the partner feature works offline.
  • Leave "Understand with Gemini" off to keep task sentences on your phone.
  • Turn off task titles in Settings → Accountability partner to share counts only.
  • Keep backup & sync off to keep your data only on your phone, or turn it on for an end-to-end encrypted copy.
  • Delete your account and all shared data in the app (Settings → Accountability partner → Delete account), or see Delete your account.

Children

Deadline is not directed at children under 13.
